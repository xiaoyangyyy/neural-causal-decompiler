# Exact abstract graph closure lowers the 128D finite-machine upper to 2,303

An executable, exact-certified finite realization for the frozen trained affine 128D ReLU ring has **2,303 states** at exact `epsilon=17/100`: 243 initial states and 2,060 recurrent states. The previous two-stage upper was 4,723. This removes 2,420 states (**51.24%** of the previous upper), while the independent 162-state packing lower leaves the same-model general minimum in **[162,2303]**.

The new construction does not use the entire 4,480-cell coordinatewise transition-image rectangle. It computes a least fixed point of *conservative abstract successors*. Exact affine extraction and rational action-cube bounds enclose all continuous actions; interval products deliberately overapproximate correlations between successor coordinates. The 243 initial states lead to 1,952 distinct recurrent candidates, and closure over recurrent transitions produces 2,060 states after enumerating 51,304 conservative edges. Each retained state is in the prior certified rectangle, and the resulting executable machine cannot transition outside the retained set.

The previous exact initial-to-recurrent handoff and weighted recurrent simulation certificates apply unchanged. The verifier recomputes the complete ordered state list from the frozen network and rejects modified evidence. These conditions cover every unit initial state, continuous action sequence, and finite time. The retained cells need not all be concretely reachable, and the **global minimum finite realization is not established**. Trained nonlinear 128D networks and the broader project requirements remain open.
